Julia wanted to know whether her offgrid cabin, deep in the countryside of Småland in Sweden, had it in her to become an Airbnb. So we asked, and the community answered louder than any of us expected: the potential is overwhelming. A quiet green wagon in a field turned out to be exactly the kind of place people are looking for.
