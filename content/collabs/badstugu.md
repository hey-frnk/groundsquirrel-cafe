Badstugu is a sauna on wheels at the lake in Ål, halfway between Oslo and Bergen, in the Norwegian mountains. We filmed a session there the way we lived it: heat, a window opening onto the water, and the long walk back through the birches. A place that needs no explaining once you have seen the light on that lake.
