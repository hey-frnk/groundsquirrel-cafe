Humbär likes nature-surrounded gravel roads, running at his own pace, beautiful scenery, and of course, old dinosaur juice. He's the oldest of us all, born in 1992 in canton Schaffhausen, where he spent his early years serving Swiss Post logistics, followed by a long chapter with a flea market dealer touring markets, before being kept by a classic car and motorcycle enthusiast. He finally found us on a snowy, dark winter day — the buses had stopped running, and we had to trudge through deep snow just to meet him for the first time. It was love at first sight.

He dreams of seeing the world and being a proper home and café wherever he goes. His dream became true in March 2025 — and since then, you find him on the road, sheltering Evelyne and Frank and sharing his cozy café on wheels with kind-hearted, wholesome people he meets along the way.
