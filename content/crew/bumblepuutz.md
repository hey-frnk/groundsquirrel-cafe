Bumblepuutz loves fields full of wildflowers, sunny rays and honey in every variation. We met Bumblepuutz after three months of being a couple, on our one-month road trip along the US West Coast. We were strolling along the seaside piers of San Francisco, watching sea lions, when Frank suddenly had a huge urge to visit a honey shop instead. It still feels like it was yesterday when Frank spotted Bumblepuutz and couldn't take his eyes off it. There was no way we'd leave the fat little bumblebee behind, so it started traveling with us, almost from the very beginning.

Bumblepuutz dreams of finding a homestead in a bee-friendly environment with lots of flowers, trees and wild animals around. Time will tell when we find that place and make Bumblepuutz's dream come true one day.
